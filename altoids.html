altoid cyberdeck

A pocket computer I designed and built inside an Altoids tin.

A Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, a Waveshare 2.8 inch touch screen, an M5Stack CardKB keyboard, a 1000 mAh battery with USB-C charging, a UDA1334A I2S audio DAC with a 3.5 mm headphone jack and a 3D printed base plate to hold it all, packed into a classic Altoids tin. Built between October and November 2026.

Drawn before building: the screen in the lid, the keyboard in the tray, how everything sits on the base plate, and every part with its size.
