about

I'm Finn, a 17 year old film maker, musician and designer.

I was born in Burbank, California, and now live in Nova Scotia. I'm part of the band Deer Jenny, make music as angel overdose, and make films. My film See Me in the Stars won the Atlantic International Film Festival for ages 18 and under, and I've been lucky enough to win Whitflix, a local film festival, a few times too.

I'm always working on new film and music projects, and I'm always looking for collaborations, so please get in touch!

My inspirations come from musicians like Elliott Smith, Iron & Wine, Alex G, Greg Mendez and Coma Cinema, and film makers like Yorgos Lanthimos, Wes Anderson and Ari Aster.
